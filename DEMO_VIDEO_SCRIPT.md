# ALUHub Demo Video Script
## 3-5 Minute Video Guide

**Total Time:** 4 minutes 30 seconds  
**Audio:** Voiceover (see notes below)  
**Format:** Screen recording + voiceover narration

---

## Audio Setup

**Recommended:**
- Use a clear, calm voiceover (male or female)
- Speak slowly and clearly (students don't speak technical jargon)
- Background music: soft, uplifting (optional, keep volume low)
- No music during voiceover — voiceover alone is best

**Recording Tools:**
- OBS Studio (free, screen + audio)
- ScreenFlow (Mac)
- Loom (simple, free tier works)
- Any screen recorder with mic input

---

## Scene Breakdown

### SCENE 1: Problem Statement (0:00 - 0:45)
**What to show:** Title slide or blank desktop  
**Audio Script:**
> "Right now, ALU students waste time filtering internships across LinkedIn, job boards, and WhatsApp. By the time you've found three good opportunities, you're tired. You apply to two roles instead of eight. And eight is how you get hired."

**On screen:** Show text or quick cuts of: LinkedIn (1 sec), job board (1 sec), WhatsApp (1 sec), clock ticking (1 sec)

---

### SCENE 2: The Solution (0:45 - 1:15)
**What to show:** ALUHub login page  
**Audio Script:**
> "This is ALUHub. One place. All ALU internship opportunities. Filtered for your year. With AI that tells you if the job fits."

**On screen:** 
- Show ALUHub login screen (3 seconds)
- Type in email: `student@alustudent.com` (2 seconds)
- Type password (2 seconds)
- Click login, wait for page to load (3 seconds)

---

### SCENE 3: Browse Internships (1:15 - 2:00)
**What to show:** Student dashboard with internship listings  
**Audio Script:**
> "You log in, set your year — Year 3 — and you see only internships for Year 3 students. No clutter. No 'Sorry, this is Year 4 only.' Just internships you can actually apply to."

**On screen:**
- Show year selector dropdown, click "Year 3" (2 seconds)
- Scroll through 3-4 internship cards slowly (8 seconds)
- Point out: company logo, job title, description, apply button
- Stop on one internship card (2 seconds)

---

### SCENE 4: AI Job Matcher (2:00 - 3:00)
**What to show:** Upload resume, see AI match scores  
**Audio Script:**
> "Now here's where it gets smart. Click 'AI Match' and upload your resume. Our AI reads it, reads the job description, and tells you exactly how good a fit you are."

**On screen:**
- Click "Use AI Matcher" button (1 second)
- Show file upload dialog (1 second)
- Drag/select a resume file (2 seconds)
- Wait for AI to process (show loading spinner) (3 seconds)
- Show results: Match score "87%" with explanation (4 seconds)
  - Point out: "87% match — strong Python skills, they need Python. But you need SQL."
- Scroll to another job, show "45% match" (2 seconds)

---

### SCENE 5: Company Research (3:00 - 3:40)
**What to show:** Click company → see AI research  
**Audio Script:**
> "Before you apply, want to know what the company actually does? Click on their name. AI researches them and tells you everything you need to know."

**On screen:**
- Click on a company name/logo (1 second)
- Show company research modal/page (1 second)
- Show AI research results:
  - Company description (2 seconds)
  - What they're known for (2 seconds)
  - Interview tips (2 seconds)

---

### SCENE 6: How to Apply (3:40 - 4:15)
**What to show:** Two apply modes  
**Audio Script:**
> "Applying is simple. For opportunities ALU posts directly, fill a quick form. For partner opportunities, one click takes you to their site. Your choice."

**On screen:**
- Go back to internship listing
- Show "Apply" button (1 second)
- **Option A (Native):** Click apply → show form filling (3 seconds)
  - Fill name, email quickly
  - Submit form
  - Show "Application submitted!" message (2 seconds)
- **Option B (External):** Click different job's apply → show external link opening (2 seconds)

---

### SCENE 7: Privacy (4:15 - 4:30)
**What to show:** Privacy settings or info section  
**Audio Script:**
> "Your data is safe. We don't sell it. We don't share it. AI reads your preferences, not your identity. You apply with confidence."

**On screen:**
- Show settings/privacy section (brief) (2 seconds)
- Show privacy checkmarks/info (3 seconds)

---

### SCENE 8: Closing (4:30 - 4:45)
**What to show:** Title slide with call-to-action  
**Audio Script:**
> "That's ALUHub. Find internships faster. Apply smarter. Get hired. Start now at aluhub.pages.dev"

**On screen:**
- Show ALUHub logo or title slide
- Show URL: https://aluhub.pages.dev
- Show demo account (optional):
  - Email: `student@alustudent.com`
  - Password: `Demo2026!`

## Recording Checklist

**Before you record:**
- ✅ Test login works
- ✅ Have a resume file ready to upload
- ✅ Know the year restrictions (which internships show for which years)
- ✅ Test AI Matcher — make sure it shows results
- ✅ Test Company Research — make sure it shows data
- ✅ Test both apply modes (in-app and external link)
- ✅ Clear desktop, close other tabs/apps
- ✅ Use zoom level that's readable (125% or 150% text size)
- ✅ Slow down your clicks and typing (narration needs time to catch up)

**Timing tips:**
- Pause for 1-2 seconds after each major action
- Let loading screens show (they're real)
- Read the script ahead of time so you know pacing
- Do 2-3 takes — you'll get better

**Audio tips:**
- Record voiceover separately (cleaner sound)
- Speak to the screen like you're showing a friend
- Use simple words: "This is...", "Now we...", "See here..."
- No technical jargon in the voiceover
