# ALUHub Internship Opportunities — How It Works

## The Problem You Face

**You know internships are everywhere, but finding them is hard.**

Right now, internship opportunities are scattered across many websites:
- LinkedIn
- Company websites
- Job boards
- Email announcements
- Partner sites

As an ALU student, you waste time filtering through all of these instead of actually **applying** and getting the internship.

---

## Our Solution: ALUHub Internships

We're building a **centralized place** where ALU school posts internship opportunities **just for ALU students**.

### What's Different?

✅ **One place to check** — all opportunities for your year in one app  
✅ **Pre-filtered for you** — only shows internships for your student year  
✅ **Apply in seconds** — no form-filling on external websites (unless the company requires it)  
✅ **AI helps you match** — our AI reads job descriptions and your profile, tells you how good a fit each role is  
✅ **AI researches companies** — our AI summarizes company background so you know what you're applying to  

---

## How ALU School Posts Internships

ALU Career Services can post internships **two ways**:

### 1. **Native Internship** (Apply on ALUHub)
- Company sends opportunity to ALU
- ALU posts it directly on our platform
- You apply **in-app** — quick form, straight to the company
- **Example:** ALU's own opportunities

### 2. **Third-Party Opportunity** (External Link)
- Company sends opportunity to ALU
- ALU posts it with a link to the company's application
- You click **"Apply"** → opens the company's website in a new tab
- You apply **on their site** using their form
- **Example:** Microsoft role that Microsoft is hosting on their own careers page

**Both types appear in your feed.** You see the company name, logo, and job details the same way. The only difference is where you click "Apply."

---

## How Other Schools Can Join

Not just ALU — any school can:
- Post internships for **their own students**
- Post internships from **partner companies**
- Restrict who sees what (Year 1? Year 3? All years?)
- Choose: apply in-app or send students to an external link

**The idea:** Education institutions decide which opportunities are right for their students, then centralize the discovery.

---

## AI Features (How We Save You Time)

### 1. **Job Matcher**
Upload your resume and interests → our AI reads each internship and tells you:
- Match score (0–100%)
- Why it's a good/bad fit
- What skills they want that you have

### 2. **Company Research**
Click on a company → our AI pulls together:
- What the company does
- What they're known for
- Quick summary (so you're ready for an interview)

---

## Getting Started

1. **Log in** as an ALU student
2. **Set your year** in your profile (Year 1, 2, 3, or 4)
3. **Browse Internships** — see only opportunities for your year
4. **Click Apply** — either fill our form (if native) or go to their site (if external)
5. **Optional:** Use AI Matcher to see which roles fit your profile best

## Questions?

- **How do I apply?** Depends on the internship. Check the "Apply" button icon — it tells you where you're going.
- **Can I see internships from other schools?** Yes, if other schools post opportunities and you match the year restriction.
- **What if I don't want to use AI?** You don't have to. Browse and apply the normal way.
- **How do companies post jobs?** Schools handle that. Companies send opportunities to ALU Career Services, and ALU posts them for you.
