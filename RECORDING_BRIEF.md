# ALUHub Demo Video — Quick Recording Brief
## For whoever is recording the video

**Total video length:** 4 minutes 30 seconds  
**What we're showing:** ALUHub internship platform — how students find and apply to internships

---

## What You'll Record

A screen recording of someone using ALUHub to:
1. Log in
2. Browse internships (filtered by year)
3. Use AI to check if a job matches their skills
4. Research a company using AI
5. Apply to an internship

---

## The Script (Just Read This)

```

job boards, and WhatsApp. By the time you've found three good opportunities, 
you're tired. You apply to two roles instead of eight.

This is ALUHub. One place. All ALU internship opportunities. Filtered for 
your year. With AI that tells you if the job fits.

You log in, set your year — Year 3 — and you see only internships for 
Year 3 students. No clutter.

Now here's where it gets smart. Upload your resume. AI reads it and tells 
you exactly how good a fit you are. Eighty-seven percent match. Here's why.

Before you apply, want to know about the company? Click their name. AI 
researches them. Tells you what they do, what interviews are like, how 
to prepare.

Applying is simple. For opportunities ALU posts, fill a quick form. For 
partner opportunities, one click takes you to their site.

Your data is safe. We don't sell it. We don't share it. You apply with 
confidence.

That's ALUHub. Find internships faster. Apply smarter. Get hired.
```

## Recording Tips

✅ **Do:**
- Speak clearly and slowly (imagine talking to a friend)
- Pause for 1-2 seconds after each action (let people see what happened)
- Move your mouse slowly across the screen
- Type slowly (people need time to read)
- Use simple words ("This is...", "Now we...", "See here...")

❌ **Don't:**
- Talk too fast
- Click/type too quickly
- Use technical jargon
- Minimize windows or switch apps
- Zoom in/out during recording
