## STEP 5: Browse Internships
**Time: 1:50 - 2:30**

On screen:

Say:
> "Here are the internships. Company logo. Job title. Description. How many apply? All of them? No. Watch this."

*Pause 1 second*

## STEP 6: Use AI Matcher (Part 1)
**Time: 2:30 - 3:15**

On screen:
- Click "AI Matcher" or "Use AI" button
- Show file upload dialog
- Select a resume file
- Show loading spinner while AI processes (let it load 3-4 seconds)

Say:
> "Click AI Matcher. Upload your resume. Our AI reads it. Reads the job description. Then tells you: how good is this match?"

*Pause 2 seconds*

---

## STEP 7: Show Match Scores
**Time: 3:15 - 3:50**

On screen:
- Show first job with match score (e.g., "87% match")
- Point out the explanation
- Scroll down, show another job with lower score (e.g., "45% match")

Say:
> "Eighty-seven percent match. Here's why: you know Python, they need Python. That's good. But you need SQL. See? Another job: forty-five percent. Too low. Why waste your time? Apply to the eighty-seven percent jobs. That's how you get hired."

*Pause 2 seconds*

---

## STEP 8: Company Research
**Time: 3:50 - 4:15**

On screen:
- Click on a company name/logo
- Show company research page or modal
- Show the research (what they do, what they're known for)

Say:
> "Before you apply, want to know what the company actually does? Click their name. AI researches them. Tells you what they do. What they're known for. What interviews are like. You walk in prepared."

*Pause 2 seconds*

---

## STEP 9: How to Apply
**Time: 4:15 - 4:35**

On screen:
- Go back to an internship
- Click "Apply" button
- Show either the form (if in-app) OR the company website opening (if external)

Say:
> "Applying is simple. Some opportunities, fill a quick form here. Others, one click takes you to their website. Either way, you apply in seconds, not minutes."

*Pause 2 seconds*

---

## STEP 10: Closing (Final 15 seconds)
**Time: 4:35 - 4:50**

Show:
- ALUHub website URL on screen: `https://aluhub.pages.dev`
- Logo or title slide (optional)

Say:
> "That's ALUHub. Find internships faster. Apply smarter. Get hired. Start now at aluhub.pages.dev"

- Don't sound like a robot
